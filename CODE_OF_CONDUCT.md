# Code of Conduct

## Purpose

safesploitOrg repositories are public engineering spaces for infrastructure, DevSecOps, automation and security-focused work.

Contributors are expected to keep discussion professional, constructive and safe for public collaboration.

## Expected Behaviour

- Be respectful and considerate in issues, pull requests, reviews and discussions.
- Focus feedback on the work, not the person.
- Provide useful context when reporting problems or requesting changes.
- Assume good intent, but correct mistakes clearly and directly.
- Respect project scope, maintainer time and repository-specific guidance.
- Avoid sharing private infrastructure details, credentials, secrets or personal information.

## Unacceptable Behaviour

The following behaviour is not acceptable:

- Harassment, intimidation, abuse or personal attacks
- Discriminatory language or behaviour
- Publishing private contact details, credentials, tokens, hostnames or sensitive infrastructure information
- Repeated off-topic, spam or bad-faith comments
- Encouraging unsafe, illegal or destructive activity
- Deliberately disrupting issues, pull requests or project maintenance

## Reporting

If you need to report unacceptable behaviour, contact the repository maintainers using the least public appropriate channel available.

If no private channel is available, open a minimal public issue asking for maintainer contact without including sensitive personal, security or operational details.

For security vulnerabilities, follow the process in `SECURITY.md` instead.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may take reasonable action to protect the project and its contributors, including:

- Asking for clarification or a change in behaviour
- Editing or deleting comments that expose sensitive information
- Closing or locking issues and pull requests
- Blocking users from further participation where necessary

Enforcement should be proportionate, consistent and focused on maintaining a safe, useful collaboration space.
